01 / FEATURES

Built to feel fast

Rotoscoped weapons

Every gun is animated frame by frame, with its own idle, fire, and reload.

Reloads that react

The shotgun's reload changes with how many shells are still in it, and each reload has its own sound.

Built to move

Sprint, jump, crouch, and slide. Staying still is how you get shot.

Kick it over

A melee kick on its own key that knocks over the fences in your way.

Kills keep you alive

Health comes from kills, so the best defense is staying aggressive.

A screen with a past

Curved glass, scanlines, and VHS tracking glitches over everything you see.

[PATROL BOT ART]
